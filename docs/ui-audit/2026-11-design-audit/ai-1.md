# AI part 1 (members, provider config, Board AI, safety & cost, schedules, where you work) — design audit

Demo video: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-kanban-board-mcp/217c9eaa-a832-52d5-8a05-ade5a07b6b57/scratchpad/audit/videos/ai-1.mp4` (not in the repo)

Summary: 53 findings (1 critical, 7 high, 30 medium, 15 low). The Board AI dock is the weakest screen: it floats over the board instead of shrinking it, so it hides header actions and columns, and it draws its icons with emoji and unicode glyphs. Across all six artboards the same three problems repeat: emoji or unicode glyphs where the design draws line icons, native `<select>` / time inputs where the design draws custom fields, and buttons that lose their icons or turn links into bordered buttons. Most states in the docs exist in code. The structure is close to the design. The gaps are mainly visual detail.

Notes on the run:
- Data and actor: the app ran as `user` (owner), and the project had one AI member, **Reviewer** (Claude Code, local runner). The design samples (seven AIs, an API-key Board AI, live costs) were not reproduced. Rows that depend on that data are compared from code.
- While I worked, another auditor (ai-2) turned **Pause all AI** on and off twice. Some shots therefore show the orange "AI is paused" banner. That state is not one of my findings.
- No AI run was started. "Run test", "Draft", "Check now", "Improve description", "Split with AI", chat send, schedule creation and "Use this AI" were never pressed.
- One side effect: pressing **Edit** on the Docs page "Overview" (to reach the editor toolbar) auto-saved an unchanged draft ("Draft · based on v1"). The published page is untouched. I left the draft in place rather than discard anything.
- Later docs extend these artboards: [ai-board-wave3](../../../design/screens/ai-board-wave3.md) ("Acts as you" replaces "AI proposes, a person applies"), [ai-team-process](../../../design/screens/ai-team-process.md) (role packs, presets in Add AI member step 3), [ai-operations](../../../design/screens/ai-operations.md) (config) and [ai-paused](../../../design/screens/ai-paused.md) (replaces safety-cost D1). Content the app has because of them is not reported as extra.

## Patterns

- **Emoji and unicode glyphs instead of line icons.** Outside comments, I counted 90 glyphs in `components/assist/` (AssistDock 16, DraftWithAi 13, AnswerBlock 11, ScopedChat 10, BoardHealth 10, AssistSettings 8) and 168 in `components/ai/` (useEditorAi 21, AiPermissionsTab 14, RunView 13, ReviewGate 11). The most common: ✓ ✕ ✦ 🔒 ⚠ 💬 📄 ⚙ ⟲ ➤ ▾ ▶ ↗ 🔍. The design always draws 11–15 px stroke SVGs. Some arrows (→) are prose and are fine.
- **Native `<select>` / date / time inputs where the design draws custom dropdowns**: about 25 places (AssistSettings, Members humans tab, Add AI member model, every config card, Inbox, Audit log, permission tester, schedule editor and list).
- **Buttons lose their icons, and links become bordered buttons**: Change, Assistant settings, Manage AI members, Open AI members page, Import/Export/Reset, Test connection, Pause, Retention, Export CSV.
- **Selected states are solid dark green** where the design uses a light green tint with a green border (tone, scope pill, permission modes, ceiling).
- **Fixed overlays and wide headers.** The Board AI dock and the hover-expanding sidebar rail both take width from a header that already holds about 12 controls, so the title wraps and New Ticket is clipped or hidden.
