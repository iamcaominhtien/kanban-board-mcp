# Ideas (Idea Space and Sketch) — design audit

Video: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-kanban-board-mcp/217c9eaa-a832-52d5-8a05-ade5a07b6b57/scratchpad/audit/videos/ideas.mp4` (not committed)

Summary: 56 findings (0 critical, 8 high, 25 medium, 23 low), plus 1 repeat of an existing finding (native `<select>`s, same as ai-1), which is not counted. The Idea Space has every feature, but most screens use a generic layout rather than the design's own components. Triage, the stage pills, the matrix, the idea-page header and the promote preview are visibly different structures. Line icons are missing almost everywhere: views, decisions, banners, activity rows and log kinds are text only, and several controls use `▾ ← × ⋯ ✦ ✓` glyphs. The Sketch tool is the exception and matches the artboard closely.

Data used: 14 ideas titled `[ideas] …` (IDEA-1 … IDEA-13 and their assumptions, evidence, votes, log entries, one comment) added through the API in KAN, MOB and "No project". Test sketches were drawn on IDEA-1, IDEA-2, IDEA-3 and IDEA-6 (while recording the video), and IDEA-5 was promoted to **KAN-38** (no AI). No AI helper was pressed. Note: on Monday after 9:00 the server's hourly digest loop wrote a "Weekly digest" for KAN on its own (17:33, shown as $0.000 with an AI chip). It was not started from the UI.

Later docs: `idea-research-log-workspace-activity.md` extends `idea-page.md` by adding three header icons, so it wins for the idea-page header. `idea-ai-help.md` "As shipped" lists switches that are on by default, Shape as its own panel and the shared Ask AI chat, so those are not findings.

## Patterns

- **Line icons missing across Idea Space** (about 12 places): view tabs, Inbox/New idea, stage pills, decision cards, dialog buttons, state banners, readiness/linked cards, log kinds, activity rows, Promote. Unicode glyphs stand in for icons in 6 places: `▾` (switcher), `←` (back), `⋯` (more), `✦` (Ask, Shape), `×` (close, delete assumption) and `✓` (vote).
- **Native `<select>`s in 11 places** (same as ai-1): see ISV-14.
- **Generic banners instead of the drawn state cards**: one-line `is-banner` with a bold lead-in replaces the icon + title + body + actions layout (IP-11, IPM-4, IAI-4, IAI-6).
- **Uppercase field labels** in dialogs and the rail (Keep, Park, Promote, Quick add, Details), where the design uses sentence case.
- **Rendered vs raw**: Promote shows raw markdown (IPM-1), and evidence and log items show raw kinds and ids where the design shows chips with icons and titles.
- **AI panels and bubbles cover content**: the Ask panel covers the header (IAI-2), and the text-AI bubble covers a fresh sketch (IP-14).
- Sketch is the reference to follow: it matches the artboard nearly 1:1.
