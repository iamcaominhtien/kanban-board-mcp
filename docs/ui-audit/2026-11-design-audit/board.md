# Board (Main Board, List, Timeline, and the larger ticket parts) — design audit

Demo video: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-kanban-board-mcp/217c9eaa-a832-52d5-8a05-ade5a07b6b57/scratchpad/audit/videos/board.mp4` (not in the repo; 1 min 41 s, 14 scenes, Vietnamese captions)

Summary: 30 findings (1 critical, 2 high, 12 medium, 15 low). The list view, the reopen dialog, the pending dialog, both card menus, the reopen/pending detail banners and the doc-reference pills, hover card and side panel are close to the design, often to the pixel. Most problems fall into three groups. (1) Width at 1440 px: the sidebar rail pushes the board, the columns refuse to shrink below 200 px, and the card header has no room once a Reopened or Pending chip is added, so the type label is cut. (2) The comment thread has broken interactions: the hover action bar never shows, and the `@` / `[[` suggesters do not open in the comment box. (3) Raw text and glyphs: the event timeline prints internal field names and values ("Changed pending set — None → None"), and emoji or unicode glyphs stand in for icons (blocked-drag ⚠️, AI card footer ⏸ ✋ ✓ ▷).

Notes on the run:
- App at 1440×900, actor `user` (owner). Project KAN.
- Data added for this audit (nothing existing was changed): KAN-30…KAN-37, all titled "[board] …". These are a bar with start and due dates and three comments plus one rich comment with a mention, a doc pill and a ticket pill (KAN-30, its description also has doc references, including a broken `[[Old rules]]`), an overdue due-only bug (KAN-31), a done ticket with dates (KAN-32), a ticket reopened twice (KAN-33), a done ticket that I reopened from the UI dialog (KAN-34), a pending-on-ticket card (KAN-35, waiting on KAN-30), a pending-on-party card (KAN-36, "Customer — Acme"), and an open ticket for the pending dialog (KAN-37, dialog opened and cancelled).
- Probes that changed nothing: the blocked drag on KAN-13 (Cancel), the reopen drop popover on KAN-34/KAN-32 (Esc), the Gantt resize on KAN-30 (dragged and released on the same day; dates checked unchanged afterwards), and the assignee picker on KAN-37 (opened and closed, nothing chosen). Text typed in the comment box was cleared and never sent. **No AI run was started**: no ticket was assigned to Reviewer, and "Ask Reviewer" and "Run" were never pressed.
- Later docs extend these artboards: [ticket-reopen](../../../design/components/ticket-reopen.md) E and [ticket-pending](../../../design/components/ticket-pending.md) F add the Reopened/Pending chips, hint lines, extra sort options and list/timeline marks; [ai-team-process](../../../design/screens/ai-team-process.md) adds the Role / Workflow / Step filters; [ai-story-flow](../../../design/screens/ai-story-flow.md) adds Group by and Give a Story. Where they replace v1 (for example the list "Sort" toggle becoming a menu), the later doc wins and the change is not reported.
- Already filed elsewhere, not repeated here: AB-2 (header overflow and the 12-item header, ai-1), AB-1/AB-3 (dock over the board), TC-1/TC-2 (card min-height, always-visible "…" button), DD-4 (drag-over outline), TS-1…TS-3 (toast size, red banner pushing the page), LD-1, and the duplicate focus ring from `docs.css` (comp-1).

## Patterns

- **Width at 1440 px is the root of several rows.** The rail pushes the board (MB-1, AB-2), columns refuse to go below 200 px, and the card header loses its type label once a Reopened or Pending chip joins the "…" button's 28 px margin (RO-1, PD-1, PD-2). Fixing the rail and the column minimum removes most of them.
- **Hover-revealed controls are hidden for good.** The comment action bar never shows (CM-2), and the comment box suggesters never open (CM-3). In both cases the code exists but is not wired to the visible state.
- **Raw internals in user-facing text.** Event timeline field names and values (TV-4), "None → None", and the "K" key stub in markers (TV-1).
- **Glyphs instead of line icons.** Blocked-drag "⚠️" (MB-5), AI footers and run panel "⏸ ✋ ✓ ▷ ↶" (AT-3), and the Type menu without icons (MB-4).
- **Reopen and pending are the closest to the design.** Their dialogs, menus, tooltips and detail banners match nearly word for word. The gaps are in the transient states (drop popover, column deltas).
