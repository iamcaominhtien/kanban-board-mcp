# Charters

One AI tester per charter, all on the commit in [`PINNED.md`](../PINNED.md). Start with the gate and the smoke charter.

| # | Charter | Persona |
|---|---|---|
| 00 | [Smoke](00-smoke.md) | Someone who just installed the app and wants to know it basically works. |
| 01 | [First run and projects](01-first-run.md) | A new user who has never seen the app, then a user with several projects. |
| 02 | [Board, List, Timeline and filters](02-board-views.md) | A person who plans and moves work all day. |
| 03 | [Ticket detail](03-ticket-detail.md) | Someone writing a careful ticket and keeping it up to date. |
| 04 | [Comments, attachments, members, activity, Recycle Bin](04-collaboration.md) | A team using the board together. |
| 05 | [Test cases, Debug Space, Workspace](05-quality-and-workspace.md) | Someone who tests and debugs a ticket and keeps files for it. |
| 06 | [Git branches and worktrees](06-git.md) | A developer who links tickets to real branches. |
| 07 | [Docs space](07-docs.md) | A writer who keeps project pages and a team that reads them. |
| 08 | [Idea Space](08-ideas.md) | A product thinker who captures ideas all day. |
| 09 | [Sketch and diagrams](09-sketch-and-diagrams.md) | Someone who draws a quick picture inside a page or a ticket. |
| 10 | [AI members, Board AI, schedules](10-ai.md) | A person setting up AI help and reviewing what it did. |
| 11 | [Settings, data and integrations](11-settings-and-data.md) | An administrator looking after the installation. |
| 12 | [Desktop app](12-desktop.md) | A desktop user. Needs a packaged build. |
| 13 | [Cross-cutting sweeps](13-sweeps.md) | A tester who looks at the whole app at once, one aspect at a time. |
| 14 | [Upgrade and data safety](14-upgrade.md) | A user who already has months of data and installs the new version. |
