1. **Create a project.** Press **+ New Project** in the sidebar and give it a name and a key (for example `KAN`).
2. **Add a ticket.** Press **New Ticket** (++Mod+N++ in the desktop app), type a title and press **Create Ticket**.
3. **Move it along.** Drag the card to **In Progress**, then **Done**.
4. **Add an AI member.** Open **Members › Add AI member** and follow the four steps ({ch:ai-members}).
5. **Connect your agent.** Open {menu:Settings › Integrations} and press **Install** for Claude Code.

## Keys you will use

++Mod+N++ New ticket · ++Mod+J++ Ask AI · ++Mod+/++ All shortcuts (desktop app)
