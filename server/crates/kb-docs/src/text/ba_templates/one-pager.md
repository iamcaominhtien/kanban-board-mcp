## Problem

What is wrong or missing today, for whom, and how we know (a number, a quote, a ticket).

## Appetite

How much it is worth: a few days, two weeks? The solution must fit it.

## Proposed solution

The core of the idea in a few lines or a rough sketch, enough to judge it, not a design.

## Success

How we will know it worked, measured: "complaints about X fall by half".

## No-gos

What we will deliberately not do, to keep it inside the appetite.

## Risks and rabbit holes

The parts that could take much longer than they look.

## Open questions and the ask

What the reader must decide, and the options.
