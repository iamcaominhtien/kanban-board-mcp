Highlight while reading
Anyone who may comment on a page can highlight it while reading, without going into edit mode, and everyone who can read the page sees every highlight. A highlight is a layer on top of the page, not part of its text: it makes no page version, survives page edits, and is not exported or printed unless asked. A note on a highlight is a comment thread. The public read-only view shows no highlights.

What a shared board taught us about passing work to a team

Six months of tickets, reports and one very patient reviewer.

ANAn Nguyen·Oct 5, 2026·6 min read

When we first let an AI team pick up a ticket, we expected the hard part to be the code. It was the briefing. A ticket that a colleague could finish from a hallway conversation turned out to need a paragraph of context,CopyCommentLinkMoreEveryone in the project who can read this page will see it. a link to the right page and a clear sense of done.

So we started writing tickets for a stranger. Each one names the goal, points to the Rate limiting page it belongs to, and lists what a reviewer will check. People noticed first: the stranger turned out to be us, three weeks later.

A ticket is a promise written for a stranger. The stranger is sometimes a team of models.An Nguyen, on the retro

Write the ticket for a stranger

A good brief fits on one screen. We settled on three short parts and stopped adding fields:

  • Goal: one sentence, in the language of the product, not the code.
  • Where: the pages and tickets that already explain the area.
  • Done: the three checks a reviewer will run.

Tracked in KAN-12, the first brief written this way shipped a week early.

Read the report, not the log

The log tells you what happened. The report tells you what the team decided and what it wants you to look at. We read the second and open the first only when something smells.

Page highlight
Part of the text. A solid fill in the page’s own highlight colour (the editor’s palette), no line, no marker. Changing it is an edit and makes a page version.
Shared highlight
A layer on top of the page. The same fill, plus a dotted line and a marker at the end, with the colour of the person who made it. It never changes the text or makes a version. Everyone who can read the page sees it.
With a comment thread
A small speech mark shows a thread. Tap the highlight to read it. A highlight keeps its place when the page is edited.

Tracked in KAN-12, the first brief written this way shipped a week early.
