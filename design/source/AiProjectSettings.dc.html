AI settings of a project

One page per project for everything around the AI teams that is not a ticket: how the teams run and what stops them, the skills and plugins they can use, what they remember, the role packs they are made from, and the code they work in. The board only sets the frame: the team decides how to work. Desktop only. Sample project: Kanban Board (KAN).
