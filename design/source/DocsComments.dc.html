Docs — comments

Confluence-style commenting on a doc page. Inline comments are attached to selected text, show as highlights, and live in a side panel as threads that can be resolved. Page comments sit at the bottom of the page. Both support replies and @mentions. Desktop and web only.
A. Page view with the Comments panel open
The Comments button in the page header toggles the panel and shows the total. Highlights carry a number that matches the card in the panel; the selected thread is stronger on both sides.

G. Rules
Proposed behavior to confirm before building. Nothing here changes how the page markdown is stored.
Anchoring
  • A comment stores the selected text, a short prefix and suffix, and its section id. The page markdown is never changed.
  • On each publish, anchors are re-matched against the new text. No match: the thread becomes orphaned.
  • Comments belong to the page, not a version. They follow the page through history and restore.
  • Selections that cross blocks (paragraph into table) are limited to one block in v1.
Who can do what
  • Viewer: read only. Member: comment, reply, resolve. Admin: also delete any comment.
  • Edit own comments any time; shown as “edited”. Delete own comments.
  • Anyone who can comment can resolve and reopen. Resolver and time are recorded.
  • Deleting a page moves its comments to the Recycle Bin with it.
Behavior
  • Panel opens from the Comments button or by clicking a highlight. Clicking a card scrolls to its highlight and back.
  • Resolved highlights disappear from the page; the thread stays in the Resolved tab.
  • Comment text supports bold, italic, code, @mentions, [[Page]] and ticket keys.
  • Unread threads get a small dot. Mentions appear in Activity and notifications.
