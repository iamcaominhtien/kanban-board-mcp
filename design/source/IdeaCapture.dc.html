Idea Space — capture & triage

How ideas get in and how you sort them. Capture is one keystroke from anywhere, and ideas can come from text you highlight or from an AI member. Triage is a short pass: keep, park, merge, or archive.

E. Rules
Proposed.
Capture
  • Anyone on the project can add an idea. AI members can add to the Inbox, nothing else.
  • An idea always records its source: a person, a Doc, a comment, a ticket, or an AI member and the ticket it was working on.
  • The Inbox button counts ideas to sort in the scope you are looking at (a project, all projects, or no project); the sidebar badge counts all of them. Neither counts parked or archived ones.
  • Quick add uses the project you are in. “No project” is always one click away, and a project can be chosen later.
Triage
  • Duplicate search runs on the title and text, against ideas, tickets and Docs. It only suggests. Merging is always your choice.
  • Every decision can be undone. Merge can be undone for 10 minutes (proposed), then it needs a manual split.
  • Parking needs a date. A parked idea with no date would never return, so one is always set.
  • Archive asks for a reason from a short list, plus an optional note. The reason is searchable.
Quiet by default
  • No notification for a new idea. You see the count on the sidebar badge and the Inbox button.
  • One optional reminder: a weekly summary of ideas waiting and parked ideas due. Off by default; set under Settings › Idea Space.
