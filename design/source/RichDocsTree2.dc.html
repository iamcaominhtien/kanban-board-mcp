Moving pages: drag, touch and select
Drag and drop reorders and re-parents. A line shows the place and the level; dropping on a page moves inside it; a closed page opens after a short hold. On touch a long-press lifts a row, and reorder mode gives handles and plain buttons for the same moves. Selecting several pages works the same way on every size.
