Screens, new — Rich Docs

Organising pages

The page tree with richer rows, the gallery and recent views, filters, drag and drop and its touch alternatives, multi-select, the row menu with its dialogs, the Recycle Bin, and the Docs space settings. Desktop is 1360, tablet 820, phone 390; menus become action sheets and dialogs become full-height sheets on a phone.
A
The page tree
Each row says what the page is: its icon or emoji, whether it is published, a draft, changed or may be outdated, how many child pages are inside, and whether AI suggestions wait. Desktop shows a cover card on hover; tablet and phone have no hover, so the same facts live in the gallery and in the row itself. Docs health and Ask the docs sit above the tree.
