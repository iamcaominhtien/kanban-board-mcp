Screen — v2

AI paused: one clear state on every screen

Two ways to stop the AI. Pause all AI stops a whole project: the top bar says so on every screen, a banner sits under it, and nothing new starts until someone resumes. Pause a team stops one team and leaves the project working. The ticket, the card and the Timeline say the same thing in the same orange. Anyone who can open the project can resume it.
A
What a pause does
Short and the same everywhere.
Project pause
Nothing new starts; a message to a team waits until you resume
Runs already going are cancelled or left to finish: you chose in the Pause all AI dialog
An answer to a team’s question can still be given
Queued runs start again, in the order they were asked, on Resume
Team pause
One team only; the project keeps working
Shown as a small amber chip “N team paused” in the top bar
Paused teams are greyed in the assignee picker
Resume from the team page or the AI teams list
Colours and words
Orange = the whole project is paused (“AI paused”)
Grey = one team paused on its own (“Paused”)
People keep working: boards, Docs and comments are never affected
