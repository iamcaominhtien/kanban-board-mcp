AI Team — AI paused: one clear state on every screen

The paused state, redesigned. The top bar and banner say who paused, when, what it did and what to do next. The same state is then drawn on Now, Flow (Team map, One ticket, Timeline), the ticket card and modal, Resume, and the states next to it. It replaces D1 of the Safety & cost screens.
A. What changes, and what does not
This board supersedes D1 of the Safety & cost screens for the banner and the top-bar state. D2 (Emergency stop), D3 (Resume), D4 (pause one AI), D5 (safe mode) and D6 (auto-trip) stay, with one small change to D3 (section C). Facts used here come from the project’s pause record: who, when, reason, runs cancelled, runs held, tokens revoked.

How the banner behaves
  • Under the top bar on every screen of the project: Board, List, Timeline, Docs, AI Team, Settings. It pushes the page down; it never floats over it.
  • Announced once to screen readers (role=status). The first button on it is Resume…; the label in the top bar is plain text.
  • Hide details folds it to one line and remembers that for you until the next pause. A new pause always opens it.
  • The numbers are the ones saved when the pause was made, so they do not change afterwards. Runs that are still finishing are counted live and the chip goes away at zero.
  • No countdown and no animation. Nothing is moving, and the page should look it.
Hover or focus on the label “AI paused”
Paused by An Nguyen at 10:412 runs cancelled, 3 queued runs held.Reason: “Unexpected assignee changes”Nothing new starts until an owner or admin resumes.
Wording
  • Paused is only used for “nothing new starts”. Held is a run waiting to start. Cancelled or stopped is a run that ended because of the pause. Resumed is the end of it.
  • A mention of an AI while paused is not delivered and is not saved for later. The ticket gets a system note (section E).
  • The Timeline view state that said “Paused: 3 new events” is renamed Scrolled back: 3 new events, so the word means one thing.
  • Safe mode is a different thing and keeps its own chip: a white chip with an eye glyph, never the solid orange.

What changes from D3
  • Every AI member row now has a sentence: what is waiting for it and what will happen. Today the row is only a name and a tick.
  • A chip on the right shows the effect in two words: starts KAN-216, waiting for you, stays paused.
  • The sentence below the list sums up the held runs in order, so the person pressing the button knows what starts.
  • A cancelled run never restarts by itself. The dialog says so on the row, because people expect it to.
What Resume does with the choices
  • AI members that are ticked start taking work again. Held runs start in order; a tick on an offline AI starts its runs when it reconnects.
  • AI members left unticked become paused one by one (reason “Not resumed after Pause all AI”). The banner goes away; the single-AI chip in the top bar appears (B1).
  • An AI that is over budget stays out; the row is greyed and says when it can start.
  • If MCP tokens were revoked, ticked members get a fresh token and reconnect on their own.
  • The safe-mode choice replaces the project setting. When safe mode was already on before the pause, the dialog preselects “Safe mode first” and says “Safe mode was on before the pause”, so choosing Normal is a deliberate step.

Rules for the ticket
  • The AI work panel always opens with the pause strip while the project is paused. Resume… is there only for owners and admins; others see the sentence instead (as in the banner).
  • The run shown is the last one, with the plain reason it ended. Run again is disabled, with the words “Available after resume”, never a silent grey.
  • The assignee picker keeps showing AI members but each is off with “AI is paused” (as in the app today), so a person cannot assign work that will not start.
  • Typing an @mention of an AI in a comment shows the amber note before sending. If sent anyway, the ticket gets the board note below the comment. Nothing is saved for later.
  • Ask AI in the ticket chat answers “AI is paused for this project” with a link to Resume… for those who can, instead of an error.

To confirm before building
  • An answer given during a pause. A run that began before the pause is not blocked by it, so BA could carry on after you answer. The screens above assume that. If you want the answer to wait for Resume, the BA card and the Needs you strip must say so.
  • Mentions made while paused are dropped with a board note. If they should wait and run on resume, the composer note and the ticket note change.
  • “Let them finish” with a very long run. The banner shows “N runs still finishing” for as long as it takes. Do we want an “also stop them” link on that chip?
  • Who can Hide details is everyone, but it is kept per person, not for the project.
  • Auto-trip “Many agents tripped” pauses the whole project. Its banner says who: “Auto-trip” in place of a name. Same layout, same buttons.
What stays the same
  • The Emergency stop dialog (D2): Cancel now or Let them finish, revoke MCP tokens, a reason. Owners and admins.
  • Pause one AI (D4), safe mode (D5) and auto-trip (D6), except that the strip and chips follow B1 and F.
  • People keep working as usual. Boards, Docs, comments and assigning to people are never affected by the pause.
  • The audit log gets the same two entries as today. The View audit log button only filters to them.
