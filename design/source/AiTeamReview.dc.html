AI Team: You stepped in and Health

Two tabs of the AI Team page. You stepped in lists every time a person had to touch the work of the teams and lets you say whether it was needed. Health says in plain words whether the teams are healthy, with five measures, a row per team and what to fix. Both only read and explain: nothing here changes a team’s settings for it. Sample project: Kanban Board (KAN), range Last 7 days, today is Sunday Nov 8.
