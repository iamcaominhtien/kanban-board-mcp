What is added to the ticket world
  • A lightbulb next to the key on cards, list rows and the timeline. Hover shows a small card with a link back.

  • A block Started as an idea on the ticket page (next section).
  • The open assumptions become a checklist that shows on the card as 0/2, like any checklist.
  • Nothing else: no new column, no new type, no new colours.

The ticket is Done

The ticket is set to Won’t do

The ticket is deleted
The idea goes back to Ready and the link is cleared. Promote again if you want. Restoring the ticket from the Recycle Bin brings the link back.
The ticket is moved to another project
The idea keeps following it. Nothing to do.

The idea is not changed. After promotion the two are independent; only the link and the status are shared.
You try to archive an idea that is Building
Not allowed while the ticket is open. Close or remove the ticket first.

I. What this means for the product (for planning)
A summary for whoever builds it. The ticket plan lives in the issue tracker.
Data
  • The idea gets: a stage (inbox, exploring, ready, building, parked, shipped, archived), a source, a park-until date, an archive reason, votes, and a link to a page body (markdown, as for Docs).
  • Assumptions become their own records: text, impact, status, optional test ticket, evidence links.
  • Evidence, drawings and comments reuse existing attachments and comments.
  • Readiness and duplicate matches are worked out on request, not stored.
API and MCP
  • REST gets stage moves, merge, park, archive, vote, promote preview, and assumption endpoints.
  • MCP stays hidden by default. When on, AI members get: add to inbox, read ideas, read assumptions. They cannot move, edit or archive.
  • Promote from an AI member is not allowed; a person must press Create.
Design rules
  • Idea Space uses the same components as the rest of the app. No separate theme.
  • Every list and page works with the keyboard first.
  • Every irreversible step has an undo or a confirmation (archive, merge, promote).
