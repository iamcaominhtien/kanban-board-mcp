Component — Rich Docs

AI teams on rich pages

A team works on a rich page the way it works on a ticket: it reads the page block by block, writes block by block, and the board shows the result. Changes arrive as suggestions you accept or reject block by block, unless the page or the team is set to apply them directly. Formatting is named (centre, float right, green), complex blocks are replaced whole, and what a team may not touch says so. Desktop first, with tablet and phone where the layout changes. Sample project: Kanban Board (KAN).
A
The page header and the change chip
When a team has worked on a page, one chip in the header says what it did and when. Where changes are waiting, the chip opens a review bar; where they were applied directly, it opens the same list with the way back.
