Idea Space — AI help

Optional help from an AI member at seven small points: capture, triage, shaping, checking, weekly grooming, asking, and preparing the ticket. It only suggests, always shows its reasons and its cost, and Idea Space works the same without it.
A. Where AI helps
Seven places, each small and optional. Every one of them only suggests. Nothing changes an idea until a person accepts. Each shows what it costs, and everything in Idea Space still works with AI turned off. The costs on this board are examples, not measured prices.

B. Triage with advice
The Inbox shows advice next to each idea: what to do, why, and how sure it is. “Accept” does exactly what a person would do with one key, and can be undone the same way. When the AI is not sure it says nothing, rather than guessing.

G. Rules
Proposed.
What AI may and may not do
  • It suggests. A person accepts, one item at a time. “Accept all” is the same as pressing each.
  • It cannot move, edit, merge, archive, delete or promote an idea by itself, including on a schedule.
  • It can add to the Inbox when working on a ticket (see Capture). It is always labelled with who and why.
  • Everything it adds is marked with the purple AI chip until a person edits it.
Cost and limits
  • Cost is shown before it runs and after. Idea Space has its own daily cap, and the AI member’s and the board’s limits apply as well.
  • Duplicate matching uses a local search, not a model, so it is free and works offline.
  • The ideas sent to a model are only the ones needed for the question.
  • Settings are per project. Ideas with no project use the workspace defaults, with Idea Space AI help limited as shown in the states.
Trust and honesty
  • Every suggestion has a reason and a confidence. When it is not sure, it says nothing.
  • Answers list the ideas, tickets and Docs they used, so a person can check.
  • Every run is in the audit log with who asked, what it read and what it cost.
  • If AI is off or fails, nothing in Idea Space is blocked.
