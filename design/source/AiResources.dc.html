AI Team: Resources

What the running AIs use on this computer, and what the system does about it. A tab with the machine, the RAM of every run, Stop and Restart; a small RAM chip on the team cards; a strip on the ticket’s run; a notice when the system restarts or stops a run. Numbers are invented but follow the real ones: a Claude Code CLI uses about 200 MB, the limit is 2 GB.
