H. Flow, whole project: Stories are the clusters
The map keeps its shape: one box per AI, one arrow per pair, the same colours and the same animation. What is new is under it. Every Story is a cluster, tickets that belong to none are one more cluster with a dashed edge, and hovering a cluster draws its share of every arrow in dark blue while the rest dims. The map answers where does work pile up; the clusters answer whose work is it. With the feed open the map is 720 px wide, which is why a box here is narrower than on main.

H2. The same screen with a lot of work
Three rules keep a month of a busy project readable: only the arrows that carry weight are drawn and the rest fold into one chip (+12 smaller hand-offs, a dashed outline that opens a list); Stories are sorted by activity and the ones that are done collapse into one faded line; tickets with no Story stay one cluster, however many there are. The scale of the arrow stays as on main: the count on it, thicker when larger.

I2. The Timeline inside a Story
On main the Timeline is one lane per AI, for every ticket in a range. Inside a Story it is one row per child ticket, with the PM on top: its bars are planning and review, its diamonds are the decisions it took for you. Bars keep their colour by the AI that held the ticket, so a ticket still reads as a hand-over in colours. The hatched amber bar and the black now line are the ones on main.

J. Flow, one ticket: the steps, then the hand-overs
The third scope is the One ticket view of main with two changes. A workflow strip goes on top: the real nine steps of Feature with a UI on main (Triage, Shape, Design, You approve the design, Build, Checks, Try it, Review, Done), who holds each, and the return from Review in the same brown dashed arc as on main. The approval is its own step; here the PM took it for you. And the feed comes back, narrowed to this ticket; on main it vanishes in this view, which is when you most want to read what the AI is doing. The ticket list on the left of main is gone, the picker replaces it.
