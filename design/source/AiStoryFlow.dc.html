Screen — v2

Story page, Group by Story, Keep awake and Flow

A Story is a ticket with sub-tickets. The board can group by Story, and a Story opens as a page that says how far its sub-tickets are and what the teams have used. Teams work on the sub-tickets like on any ticket. Flow, the second tab of AI Team, draws the whole project, one team, one Story or one ticket as a Map or a Timeline: its scope bar, its Map and its empty states come after the Story page.
