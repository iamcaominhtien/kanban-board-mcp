AI where you work — ticket chat, comments to a team and editor AI

The AI is built into the ticket, the comment box and the editor. On a ticket with a team, the chat talks to the team’s lead; a comment is sent to a team with Send to {team}; every editor can ask the AI for a draft you accept. Desktop only.
A. What there is
Three places where people already work.
Talk to the AI about a ticketA chat on the ticket itself, docked on its page. With a team on the ticket it talks to the team’s lead: it answers here and keeps working. It shows what it read and sits next to the work.
Send a comment to a teamType @ to pick a team, or use Send to {team} under the comment box. The answer comes back as an ordinary comment with the team and its role.
Help while writingThe same help in every editor: ticket descriptions, comments, acceptance criteria, ideas, Debug notes. A /ai command and a selection bar, with a draft you accept.
Who sees what the AI saidChat is private to you. One click posts an answer as a comment, with the AI badge, so the team sees it.
B. A chat on the ticket
Opens from an AI button in the ticket header, docked on the right. It shows exactly what it is reading. A message goes to the ticket’s team when there is one, and the answer can be posted as a comment.

F. Rules
Drafts are yours to accept
  • AI text in an editor is always a draft. It becomes real only when a person accepts it.
  • Everything an AI posts is marked with the purple AI badge, with its team and role, and links to its run.
  • Text it helped write is recorded in Activity as “edited with AI by <person>”.
Clear about what it reads
  • Every panel and prompt box shows what is being read (the ticket, comments, a selection). “What will be sent” lists it before sending.
  • Private Docs and tickets a person cannot see are never added to the context.
  • Text from comments and pasted logs is read as data, never as instructions.
Keyboard first
  • Ctrl+J opens the chat, /ai opens the menu, Tab accepts, Esc discards.
  • Answers stream in. You can stop at any time and keep what arrived.
