AI where you work — ticket chat, @mentions and editor

AI built into the ticket, the comment box and every editor, using the AI members, limits and audit that already exist. You can chat on the ticket, call an AI with @ and choose whether it only answers or does the work, and get help while writing as a draft you accept.
A. What exists and what this board adds
The AI boards already cover AI members, the Board AI dock, safety and cost. This board brings AI to where people are working: on the ticket, in the comment box, and in the editor. It reuses the same AI members, permissions, budgets and audit log; nothing here is a new kind of AI.

B. A chat on the ticket
Opens from an AI button in the ticket header, docked on the right, like Debug and Test. It is already scoped: it shows exactly what it is reading. It can answer, draft (as drafts you apply), post an answer as a comment, or, in “Do the work” mode, start a run. The conversation is saved on the ticket and is private until you share an answer.

F. Rules
Proposed.
Safe by default
  • A question never starts a run. Ask only is the default; Do the work is chosen on purpose and shows its limits.
  • AI text in an editor is always a draft. It becomes real only when a person accepts it.
  • Everything an AI posts is marked with the purple AI badge and links to its run. Text it helped write is recorded in Activity as “edited with AI by <person>”.
  • AI cannot approve itself, cannot @mention another AI to widen its rights, and obeys the existing safe mode and approval rules.
Clear about what it reads
  • Every panel and prompt box shows what is being read (the ticket, comments, a selection). “What will be sent” lists it before sending.
  • Private Docs and tickets a person cannot see are never added to the context.
  • Text from comments and pasted logs is fenced as data, as set in the safety rules.
Cost and speed
  • Cost is shown before sending and on every answer. Ticket and editor helpers count toward the AI member’s daily cap and the board’s.
  • Answers stream in. You can stop at any time and keep what arrived.
  • Keyboard first: Ctrl+J opens the chat, /ai opens the menu, Tab accepts, Esc discards.
