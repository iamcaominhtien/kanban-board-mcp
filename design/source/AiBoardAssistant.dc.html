Board AI — assistant and board health

The Board AI is a Claude Code session of the project’s first team. It answers questions about the board and acts: what can be undone it does at once and says what it did; what is hard to undo (delete, remove a member, pause every AI, merge) it describes and asks first. A bar shows how full its conversation is. On a ticket that has a team, the ticket chat talks to that team’s lead. Desktop only. Sample project: Kanban Board (KAN).
