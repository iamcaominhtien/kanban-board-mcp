AI teams — list, add and team page

AI teams sit next to people: they can be assigned tickets and @mentioned. A team is a lead plus roles, all Claude Code, running as a Claude account chosen when the team is added. This board covers the Members page (Humans, AI teams, AI accounts), the Add an AI team dialog and the team page. The board only shows and records the AI’s work: it holds no gate on it. Desktop only. Sample project: Kanban Board (KAN).
A
Entry points
The sidebar button Members opens the project’s member panel. Its AI tab is a quick view: the first four teams, their account and status, and the way to the full page. No team picks which AI answers board-wide requests.
