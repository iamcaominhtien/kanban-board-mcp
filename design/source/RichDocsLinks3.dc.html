Rate limiting

Published · v15HMEdited by Hoa Mai · 2h ago

Overview

Every route counts requests per token in a sliding one-minute window. When a token goes over its limit the API answers 429 with a Retry-After header. See Auth for how tokens are issued.

Rate limits are not enforced yet. Tracked in KAN-12.

What fixes do
Relink and Use change the target of the link; the text you wrote stays
Restore brings the page back; the link works again at once
Remove link keeps the words and drops the link
What it never does
It never deletes a page or a block
Nothing changes until you press a button; Undo stays for 10 seconds
