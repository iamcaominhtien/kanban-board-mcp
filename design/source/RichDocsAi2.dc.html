Why we moved our roadmap into the board
Notes from two years of planning in slides
An Nguyen · 6 min read
Planning night in the Hanoi office

We kept the roadmap in a slide deck for two years. Every quarter someone exported it, every sprint someone forgot to update it, and by the third week nobody trusted it.

Moving it into the board did not make planning easier, but it made the answer to “what are we building?” the same for everybody. The first release on the board shipped on ⚠ Needs input: date of the first release.

“By the second month the pages were doing the work the meetings used to do.”

What surprised us most was how little the tooling mattered once the habit was there. A shared place to write things down, and a promise to read it before asking.
