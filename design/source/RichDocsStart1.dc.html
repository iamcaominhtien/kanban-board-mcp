Starting and shaping a page

How a page begins and how it is set up: the empty space, the new page dialog with its template gallery, page settings (width, fonts, colours, cover, what shows), saving a page as a template, importing from other tools, and the first look at a page that was converted from Markdown. Desktop is 1360, tablet 820, phone 390; dialogs become full-screen sheets on a phone.

First visit and the empty space
Nothing yet, so the page offers a start. The start shows what the editor can do: posts, chapters and stories next to specs. Templates show real blocks, and importing is one tap away.
