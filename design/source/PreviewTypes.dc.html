HTML opens as the real page, not as code, so reports, exported pages and mockups look right. It is shown in a locked frame: no scripts, no outside requests, no forms or pop-ups. Source is one click away, and it can be viewed at desktop, tablet or phone width.

I. Spreadsheets and CSV
Opens as a table, not a download. Excel is drawn as Excel shows it: fills, fonts, borders, number and date formats, merged cells, frozen rows and columns, colour scales, data bars, charts and pictures, one sheet at a time with tabs. Rows come in pages of 500 with Show more; a CSV keeps its first 500 rows. Formulas show their last saved result; none is run.
