Screen — v2

AI accounts — sign in once, share between teams

A list of AI accounts (one sign-in of Claude Code), a sign-in window with a real terminal that tells you what it is waiting for, choosing an account when creating or editing an AI team, and how teams show their account and what happens when it is signed out. Desktop only.
A
Where each piece lives
Desktop only. An AI account is one sign-in of Claude Code. Several AI teams can pick the same account: sign in once, and when it expires fix it in one place. Only a person can sign in; an AI has no way to open or use the terminal. The terminal text on this board is illustrative.

D
The sign-in window, moment by moment
Above the terminal there is always one sentence that says what is happening and, in amber, when it is your turn; blue means the app or the browser is working. Three dots show roughly where you are. The terminal is the real thing and stays usable; the sentence is a best-effort reading of it. Done is decided by asking the CLI who it is signed in as, never by reading the screen.
