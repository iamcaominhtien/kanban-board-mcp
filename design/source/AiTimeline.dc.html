AI Team › Flow — the Timeline

A sequence diagram of the AI teams, and the second view of Flow, the second tab of AI Team (the switch reads Map | Timeline). You, each team’s lead and each role the lead called are lanes, and a header over each team groups them; time runs down; a bar is a run or one stretch of work of a role; an arrow is an assignment, a role call, a note from one team to another, a question or an answer. It shows who did what and when, where the waiting is, and what is happening now. The scope bar above it says what is drawn: all teams, one team, one Story or one ticket. The same picture, scoped, sits in a ticket. The frames that show the whole page carry the tab strip; the others are excerpts of the diagram.

Where it livesThe tab Flow of AI Team, second view of its switch Map | Timeline. One scope bar sits above both views.The Map shows who is connected to whom; the Timeline shows when. One scope, two pictures, one click apart.
Shape: lanes as columns, time downwardTime runs down and the newest event is at the bottom, where a live view follows it.A live view reads like a log. Labels stay horizontal, and a day of work scrolls naturally.
LanesYou, then each team’s lead followed by the roles it called, in order of first call. A lead is a dark box like You; a role box says the role only, in its own colour.You talk to the lead. The roles are its helpers, so they sit beside it.
Team headersA band over each team’s lead and roles, in the team’s colour, with its name and “N roles”. A click folds the roles under the lead and opens them again. Only the roles fold, never the team or its lead.With many teams the lanes are many; fold the team you do not follow and keep the others open.
ScopeAll teams, one team, one Story or one ticket, picked in the scope bar. Only the lifelines that took part are shown. The Map and the Timeline keep the same scope.A ticket’s own Timeline is the same picture, filtered. A ticket and its Story open Flow on it with “Open in Flow”.
RangeThe Timeline has its own range: Last hour, Today, Last 24 h, 7 days, Custom…. A Story or a ticket opens it on 7 days.7 days is the longest preset, the nearest to the whole life of a ticket. The Map has its own range.
Long spans and quiet stretchesCompact time folds a stretch with nothing running into one labelled break. A wait is never folded to nothing. Real time shows true proportions.Quiet time carries no information; waiting is the whole point.
How much shows at onceGlance: bars with a ticket key. Hover: a small card. Click: a side inspector. Detailed adds step ticks.Everything beyond who, when and how long is one move away; the keyboard reaches the same.
Colours and symbolsHatched grey: waiting for an AI or a runner. Hatched amber: waiting for you. + assigned, › role call, ↔ note to another team (teal), ↵ report back, ? asks you, ✓ your answer.Every state has a shape or a glyph as well as a colour.
Narrow windowsLanes keep a minimum width and the diagram scrolls sideways; headers and time gutter stay put. Teams with nothing in the range fold into one strip.Scrolling keeps every lane readable.
SharingCopy link sits in the scope bar: it opens this scope and this view. The range and the switches are not in it.A link says “look at this team or ticket”; the stretch is one click on the toolbar.
MotionOnly what is live moves: a running bar, the now line, a role call under way, a question that waits. When the AI is paused nothing moves.If everything moves, nothing does.
