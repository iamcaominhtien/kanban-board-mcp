Screen — v2

AI Team

What each team is doing, its runs and its roles. A project that has an AI team gets AI Team in its top bar, next to Board, List, Timeline and Docs. Seven tabs: Now, Flow, Run history, You stepped in, Health, Resources and Memory. Flow draws how work moves between you, the teams and the tickets, as a Map or a Timeline. The board only shows the work: nothing on this page holds back or decides what a team does. Sample project: Kanban Board (KAN).
