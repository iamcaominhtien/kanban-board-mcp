The AI sets up the project’s commands

In Settings › AI › Repositories, an AI can find the prepare, test, lint and build commands: it reads the repository, tries each command once in a temporary copy of it and shows its evidence. You can type them instead, at any time. The commands are what worktrees, tickets and teams run; the board never turns them into a gate on the AI. Desktop only.
A
Where it lives
Settings › AI › Repositories. The four commands are boxes you can type in at any time; one button asks an AI to fill them in with proof. Nothing here is a condition for a ticket: a team runs the commands the way it sees fit.
