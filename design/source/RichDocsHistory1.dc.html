Component — Rich Docs

History and comments on rich pages

Versions are compared as the page looks, block by block: added, removed and changed blocks, formatting changes named in words, tables and embeds as whole objects, images side by side. Comments attach to a block and a quote inside it, so they survive formatting changes, and they can sit on images, tables and embeds too. Desktop first, with tablet and phone where the layout changes. Sample project: Kanban Board (KAN).

Version history
A page’s versions in a drawer: who wrote each, when, with what note, whether AI text was accepted, and how many blocks changed. Any version can be viewed, restored, or copied as a new page.

What changed

We kept the roadmap in a slide deck for two years. Each quarter somebody exported it, each sprint somebody forgot to update it.

Planning night in the Hanoi office

The first month was the hard one. Tickets had to be small enough to finish and big enough to matter, and each epic needed a page that explained the why. By the second month the pages were doing the work the meetings used to do. What surprised us most was how little the tooling mattered once the habit was there.

“By the second month the pages were doing the work the meetings used to do.”
