AI Team › Flow — the Timeline: time, scope, motion and states

The details behind the Timeline view of Flow: how long and quiet stretches are drawn, how the scope bar picks all teams, one team, one Story or one ticket, what moves while it is live, every state, and where the data comes from.
E
Long spans and quiet stretches
Time is the whole diagram, so how it is stretched matters: compress what carries no information (quiet time) and keep what does (waiting).
1. Quiet stretches fold — Compact time: a stretch with nothing running becomes one labelled break; the clocks on either side stay
2. Real time — the same hour and a half with true proportions: the quiet stretch is a long empty gap
3. A wait is never folded to nothing — a very long wait is drawn capped, with a break glyph across it, and says its exact length
4. The range at a glance — the strip above the diagram: bars of how much ran; hatched where nothing ran. Drag the box to move it, an edge to zoom; zoomed in, the range reads “10:10 – 10:40”
5. Seven days — over a day the diagram is replaced by a band per day: when runs started, how many failed and how many waited long

G
What moves, and when
Only what is live moves. The Timeline is open for long stretches, so motion is small, slow and tied to meaning: this is running, this just arrived, this waits for you. Everything finished is still.
1. The now line and a running bar — the bar grows with the line; its head pulses while it runs; the dot on the line pulses too
2. A new run arrives — it appears queued: hatched grey, with how long it has waited once over 10 minutes; when a slot frees it fills and starts
3. A role call under way — the arrow is dashed and moves while the role has not started; it turns solid when the next bar starts
4. A question waits — the bar turns hatched amber, a ripple runs on the question mark and on the dot of the You box
5. A run fails with a role still open — its bar flashes red once and ends with a cross; the role’s span is closed at the same moment
6. An arrow at the now line — an arrow at the same time as now is drawn 16 px above the line; a clock label within 14 px of now is left out
7. A note from another team — the teal arrow is dashed and moves while the run it starts is queued; it turns solid when that run starts
8. Following and scrolling back — the view follows now unless you scroll up; then a pill counts what arrived

Where the data comes from
Everything on the diagram is built from records that already exist, so the Timeline adds a view, not a new kind of tracking.
ON THE DIAGRAMWHAT IT IS MADE FROMWHERE IT COMES FROM
Run bar of a leadqueued, started and ended times, state, ticket, run number, steps, tokens, cost, model, summary, whether it was stopped by a person, the pause, a budget or memoryai_run
A role’s spanone bar per stretch of work: it starts when the lead called the role and ends when the role reported back; a role called twice has two spans; a span open when the run ends is closed at the endai_run_agent and its role markers in ai_run_step
Assigned arrowthe run’s trigger (assign, run button, a message, a retry) with who started it; a schedule, a GitHub event or the API draws no arrowai_run
A note to a running leadan arrow from you to the lead with the first words of the noteai_run_message (direction in)
A note from one team to anothera run started by a note (message or ai_message) whose sender is the name of another team of the project: a teal arrow from that team’s lead, at the time the run was queued, to the time it started; a sender that is no team (the board’s own agent) draws no arrow, and the sender’s lane is drawn even when it had no run in the rangeai_run (trigger, started by)
Team headerthe teams on the diagram, each with its lead and the roles it called; “N roles” counts the role lanes the server returned for that teamlifelines of the Timeline read
Role call and report backthe start and the end of each span of a roleai_run_agent
Report to youa run that finishedai_run
Question and answerthe question, when it was asked, when it was answered, who answeredai_run (question)
Queued bar, held bara queued run is a hatched bar from its queue time; held when the whole project is pausedai_run and the AI settings
Pause bandwhen the whole project was paused, and the reasonthe AI settings of the project
Scopeall teams, one team (its lanes are drawn even when nothing ran), one ticket, or a Story: the runs of its sub-ticketsthe scope bar: a team id or a ticket id as a parameter
State dot of a lanea team’s connection, whether it is paused, its live runs; a role is working while one of its spans is openteams, ai_run
What the app needs
One read for the Timeline: the range and the scope (a team, a ticket or a Story) as parameters; it answers with bars, arrows, waits and lane states
Close arrows come grouped (within 90 seconds, within 10 minutes when there are over 500); at most 400 runs and 500 arrows
A run’s steps are read when the inspector opens, not with the diagram
Reading it without a mouse or without colour
The Table toggle lists the same events in order for screen readers and for copying
Tab moves between the marks; Enter opens the inspector, Esc closes it, Home and End jump to the top and to the now line
Every state has a shape or a glyph as well as a colour (hatch, cross, question mark, pause); times are in your time zone
