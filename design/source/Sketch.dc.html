Sketch — a paint-style drawing tool

A simple, offline drawing tool that works like a basic paint program: brush, pencil, eraser, fill, shapes, text and a few layers. It starts from a blank page or from any picture, and the result can be placed in any text, ticket, Doc or idea. Diagrams (draw.io) stay for structured drawings.
A. Where you start a drawing
A drawing is one object that works everywhere pictures work: in text, on a ticket, in a Doc, on an idea. It always starts from an obvious place and always comes back to where you were.

C. The tools
Ten tools, the ones a simple paint program has. One-key shortcuts. Everything else (blend modes, filters, curves) is left out on purpose.
Select and moveV
Drag a box, then move, resize or delete what is inside.
PencilP
Thin hard line. Best for writing and outlines.
BrushB
Soft line with size, softness and opacity. Responds to pen pressure.
EraserE
Rubs out on the current layer. Same size and softness options.
FillG
Fills a closed area with the colour. Has a tolerance setting.
Pick colourI
Click any pixel to use its colour. Hold Alt while drawing for a quick pick.
Line and arrowL
Straight lines, arrows and curves. Hold Shift to snap to 15 degrees.
Rectangle and ellipseR
Outline or filled. Hold Shift for a square or circle.
TextT
Click and type. Size and colour like any other. Stays editable until you merge layers.
Crop and resizeC
Change the canvas size, crop to a selection, or trim to the content.

E. What is stored and how it behaves
Proposed.
Two files, one drawing
  • A picture (PNG) that is shown in text, lists and previews. It works everywhere an image works, including exports.
  • An editable source with the layers, so the drawing can be opened again and changed later.
  • Both are attachments of the page that owns them, so they are backed up and exported like any attachment.
Saving
  • Done saves and returns to where you were. Everywhere the drawing is shown updates.
  • Save a copy makes a second drawing and leaves the first one alone.
  • A draft is kept on this computer every few seconds, so a crash or closing the window does not lose work.
  • Limits: 4096 × 4096 px, 10 MB, 10 layers. Past that, the editor says so before it fails.
Offline, safe, quiet
  • Works fully offline. No online service is used. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • No sound or animation unless you turn it on. Respects reduced motion.
  • Undo goes back at least 100 steps and survives switching tools.
F. Pen, touch and keyboard
The tool must work with a mouse, a pen tablet and a touch screen.
Pen
  • Pressure changes the size or the opacity (your choice in the options bar). Off by default for a mouse.
  • The pen’s eraser end, if it has one, switches to the Eraser while held.
  • Palm rejection: while a pen is near the screen, touches are ignored.
Touch
  • One finger draws. Two fingers move and zoom the canvas. A two-finger tap undoes; a three-finger tap redoes.
  • On a narrow screen the tool bar moves to the bottom, and Colour and Layers become sheets opened from buttons.
  • Buttons are at least 44 px high on touch devices.
Keyboard
  • CtrlZ undo, CtrlShiftZ redo, [ ] brush size, Space hold to pan.
  • CtrlS saves, Esc asks to save if needed.
  • Tools have one-letter shortcuts shown on hover. All options can be reached without a mouse.
G. Sketch or Diagram?
Both stay. They solve different problems, so the menu names them plainly and says what each is for.
Choose Sketch when…
  • You are thinking, not documenting: a rough layout, a shape, a note on a screenshot.
  • You want to draw freely with a pen or finger.
  • You are marking up a picture (circle the bug, point at the button).
Choose Diagram when…
  • You need boxes joined by arrows that stay joined when you move them.
  • Someone else will edit it later, or it must open in draw.io.
  • It is a flow, an architecture, a data model.
What we are not building
  • No photo editing: no filters, curves, blend modes or RAW support.
  • No real-time drawing together. One person edits at a time, as with Docs.
  • No cloud. Everything stays in your data folder.
