## A. You stepped in: every manual touch, and which ones were needed

Every time a person had to act, in order, with the AI and ticket it was about. Each touch carries one of three marks. By rule: the project is set up to ask for it (approvals, Done). Needed: only a person had the information or judgement. Avoidable: a setting, a note in the instructions or an automatic retry would have removed it. These marks are the answer to “how autonomous is the team”, and each one says why. Click a row to open the run, the approval or the audit entry behind it.
